### Case study: Chicago Residential

- Facts: Developer and builder LG Group · US$35M · 230 units · 160,000 sq ft · 15 subcontractors · October 2025 to now
- The job: Keep a 230 unit build on programme while materials, trades and cost all move at once.
- What I do:
  - Own the programme and update it against two site days a week, so slippage shows up from field conditions before it reaches the critical path. Close each week with an open items list and a progress report.
  - Run the procurement log for about 250 packages: submittal approval, fabrication lead time and delivery against the required on site date, with follow ups two to three weeks before each activity.
  - Hold the finishes, roofing and steel packages from buyout to installation.
  - Administer variation orders (12 so far) within an authority of 1 to 2% of contract value; keep the cost log and contingency forecast.
  - Kept crews working through a switchgear delay by resequencing conduit, cable tray and branch electrical work, tracking factory release and shipping daily. The permanent power milestone held.
  - Collect EPDs, product data, recycled content and waste records, and package the contractor side sustainability submission.
- Results (number tiles): 97% material readiness · 30% fewer delayed activities than the baseline programme · GMP drift held within 1% · US$18k saved on a US$300k lighting package

### Case study: Holiday Inn Express

- Facts: SCI Group, general contractor · US$15M · 110 keys · five storeys
- What I did: subcontractor sequencing, drawing and RFI reviews, procurement tracking and resolution of site issues.
- Across SCI projects: set up a central open issues register that cut coordination turnaround from four days to three; caught trade conflicts such as ductwork against ceiling layouts on paper, and repeat field corrections fell 40%; tracked 150+ snagging items to closeout.
- The job (from Vedant): Managed project execution from initiation through final handover, coordinating construction activities, project stakeholders and multidisciplinary teams.
- Added to What I did (from Vedant): BIM coordination to find and resolve clashes between architectural, structural and MEP systems before they reached site. Design reviews with the project teams that reduced coordination clashes.
- Dates: Vedant didn't give them, so the page has none.

### Case study: Staybridge Suites

- Facts: SCI Group as owner's representative · Bradley, Illinois · June 2024 to August 2025

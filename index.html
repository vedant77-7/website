A-01/ Method

How a project gets from a brief to a building, and where I keep it on track.

  1. 01

    Concept

    Every open design question gets an owner and a date, so answers keep pace with the brief.

  2. 02

    Design

    Drawings checked against code, fire NOC and every consultant’s comments in one pass.

  3. 03

    Procure

    Tenders compared scope by scope; long lead items tracked to the day they reach site.

  4. 04

    Build

    Programme updated from site twice a week, so slippage shows before it hits the critical path.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Snags closed, closeout records and sustainability submissions packaged.
